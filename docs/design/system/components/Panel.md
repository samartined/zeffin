A panel that unfolds from the app bar over a 60% black scrim that leaves the bar uncovered: `surface-raised`, radius 16 on the bottom corners, title in `heading` with an optional subtitle, and below it settings rows (`SettingRow`), the effort dial (`EffortDial`) or a list of choices. A list opened from the panel shows an arrow before its title that returns to the panel. The content scrolls when it does not fit, leaving at least 48 dp of scrim visible. It closes by tapping the scrim, the bar's button that opened it, or Back (a list returns to the panel first). Used for the conversation settings, opened from the app bar.
