The Flipper screen: outer 3px `screen-bezel` frame with `radius-lg`, a 6px gap and the orange `screen-paper` LCD with a 12px radius and 8px padding, with the `screen-pixel` pixels in black. The 128x64 framebuffer is drawn at an integer scale without smoothing. The consumer passes `frame` (the 1024 bytes from the device, packed vertically) and `scale`; in the app the scale is the largest that fits the available width. With `stale` the image dims to 40% and a `warning` pill says why (`staleText`: 'Stale screen · 14 s' or 'Offline'); when offline the LCD turns `line`.
