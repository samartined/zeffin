A bottom sheet over a 60% black scrim: `surface-raised`, radius 16 on the top corners, gray handle, title in `heading` with an optional subtitle, and below it settings rows (`SettingRow`) or action rows (`ActionRow`), with an optional primary button at the end. Used to filter and sort a list and for the actions on an item (pin, favorite, rename, remove). It closes by tapping the scrim or with Back, which closes the sheet and not the screen under it. The conversation settings use a Panel instead, from the app bar.
